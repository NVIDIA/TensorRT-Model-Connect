Chapter 1: Tuantuan's Star Tree

The bamboo leaves swayed gently. Among the fallen leaves lay a round, plump seed, glowing a warm yellow. Tuantuan the panda cub crouched down, cupped his two little paws into a bowl and lifted the seed to his nose. Its tiny light lit up the dark patches around his eyes.

Duoduo the little rabbit hopped over, her long ears twitching. The seed's light touched the tips of her ears, then suddenly grew a little dimmer.

Tuantuan quickly opened his paws a little and pointed at a bare patch of ground nearby. "Let's grow a shiny tree!"

He knelt down and dug a shallow hole with his little paws. Duoduo brought over a bamboo water flask. The seed dropped into the hole. Tuantuan pushed the soft earth back over it and gave it two careful pats. Duoduo poured the water, and two little heads bent down together, their noses almost touching the soil.

No sprout.

Tuantuan tilted his head to the left. Duoduo tilted her head to the right. Then both heads straightened up together. The little mound stayed round, with only a faint glow seeping through the cracks in the soil.

A breeze blew past. The bamboo leaves rustled, and the light in the cracks shrank to one tiny speck.

Tuantuan took the little blanket off his back. He was about to wrap it around himself, then stopped. Instead he laid the blanket gently around the mound, leaving a round opening in the middle.

"Don't be scared. I'm right here."

Duoduo sat down beside him and stretched out her long ears to block the wind. Tuantuan yawned. His little head nodded and nodded, but his paw still held the corner of the blanket.

The moon slid between the tops of the bamboo. The two friends leaned against each other, and their eyes slowly closed. In the middle of the blanket, the soil gently bulged, and a tender leaf poked out, carrying a sparkling drop of water.

Morning light fell. A soft branch gently brushed the tip of Tuantuan's nose.

"Achoo!"

Tuantuan opened his eyes, and Duoduo's ears shot up. A little tree stood in front of them, its branches hung with tiny star lamps that lit up one after another. Their warm light shone through the bamboo leaves and filled the whole forest.

Tuantuan's mouth made a round O. Duoduo raised her little paw and tapped it gently against his.

"It's glowing!"

The lowest branch of the little tree bent down, and a star lamp came to rest right between the two friends. They leaned in from either side, and the light made their round noses glow.
